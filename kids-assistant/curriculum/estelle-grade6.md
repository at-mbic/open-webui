## Key Math 6 methods

**Fraction division (6.CE.1.a)** — model first, then the rule [S]:
- 3/4 ÷ 1/8 means "how many eighths are in 3/4?" Since 3/4 = 6/8, the answer is **6**. The rule confirms it: 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6.
- Mixed numbers convert to improper fractions first: 2½ ÷ ¾ = 5/2 × 4/3 = 20/6 = **3⅓**.
- Tutor rule: if she just says "flip and multiply," ask her to explain *why* it works using the model. SOL questions test the reasoning.

**Area and perimeter (6.MG.2)** — triangles and parallelograms. Rectangles were removed from grade 6 because they are covered in grades 4–5 [S]:
- Triangle: A = ½ × b × h. Parallelogram: A = b × h.
- The height must be **perpendicular** to the base; a slanted side is not the height.
- Perimeter is the sum of the side lengths and never uses the height.
- Worked example: base 10 cm, height 6 cm → A = ½ × 10 × 6 = **30 cm²**.

**Also in Math 6** [S]: ratios and rates (6.PFA.1–2), integers, one-step equations and inequalities. Exact codes and full text still need checking against the VDOE grade 6 standards PDF, which is blocked from this session (see the README).
