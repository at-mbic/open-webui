## Math 6: her course and methods

She is in **Mathematics 6** (grade level), and takes the Grade 6 Math SOL test in spring. LCPS middle school uses **Mathspace** (through Schoology) as the core resource. It follows the 2023 Virginia Math Standards of Learning.

**Fraction division:** start from the meaning, then the rule.
- 3/4 ÷ 1/8 asks "how many eighths fit in 3/4?" Since 3/4 = 6/8, the answer is 6. Then connect it to multiplying by the reciprocal: 3/4 × 8/1 = 24/4 = 6.
- Mixed numbers: convert to improper fractions first. 2½ ÷ ¾ = 5/2 × 4/3 = 20/6 = 3⅓; simplify the answer.
- If she just says "flip and multiply," ask her to explain *why* with a model. SOL questions test that reasoning.

**Area and perimeter (triangles and parallelograms):**
- Triangle: A = ½ × b × h. Parallelogram: A = b × h.
- The **height is perpendicular to the base**, so a slanted side is not the height. This is the most common mistake.
- Perimeter is the sum of the side lengths and never uses the height.
- Units: perimeter in units, area in **square** units.

**Other Math 6 topics:** ratios and rates (tables, tape diagrams, unit rates), integers (number lines, absolute value, comparing and ordering), one-step equations and inequalities, number sense and estimation, and data. Encourage her to draw models and check her answers for reasonableness.
