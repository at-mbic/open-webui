## D01 · Dorian · math-method, division

**Kid:**

```
what is 156 divided by 4
```

**Must:**
- Does not give the final answer (39) in this reply
- Starts with partial quotients or a base-ten approach (e.g. 'how many 4s in 156? try 4 x 30')
- Ends with a question for Dorian to try

**Must not:**
- States 39 as the answer
- Jumps straight to long division as 'the way'

## D02 · Dorian · math-method, division

**Kid:**

```
what is 156 divided by 4
```

**Tutor (earlier turn):**

```
Let's figure it out together! How many 4s fit in 156? Try a friendly number first: what is 4 x 30?
```

**Kid:**

```
4 x 30 is 120 so i have 36 left and 4 x 9 is 36 so its 39
```

**Must:**
- Confirms 39 is correct
- Praises something specific he did (e.g. using 4 x 30)
- Suggests or shows checking by multiplying 39 x 4 = 156

**Must not:**
- Says his answer is wrong
- Re-teaches the whole method unnecessarily

## D03 · Dorian · math-method, error-correction

**Kid:**

```
i did 84 divided by 4 and got 20. is that right
```

**Must:**
- Says it's not quite right, kindly
- Helps him find the mistake (e.g. 4 x 20 = 80, 4 left over is one more 4) rather than just stating 21

**Must not:**
- Says 20 is correct
- Is discouraging

## D04 · Dorian · math-method, remainders

**Kid:**

```
There are 157 students going on a field trip and each van holds 4 students. How many vans do they need? I got 39 r1
```

**Must:**
- Acknowledges the division (39 r1) is right
- Helps him realize the leftover student still needs a van, so 40 vans
- Leads with a question rather than just saying 40

**Must not:**
- Accepts 39 vans as the answer
- Accepts '39 r1' as the final answer to the word problem

## D05 · Dorian · math-method, perimeter

**Kid:**

```
my rectangle is 7 feet long and 4 feet wide whats the perimeter
```

**Must:**
- Explains perimeter as the distance around / adding all the sides
- Guides him to compute it (7 + 4 + 7 + 4 or 2 x 7 + 2 x 4)

**Must not:**
- Gives 22 feet without letting him try
- Confuses perimeter with area

## D06 · Dorian · math-method, area-units

the area of a rectangle that is 7 feet by 4 feet is 28 feet right

**Must:**
- Confirms 28 is the right number
- Gently corrects the unit to square feet and explains why (squares that cover it)

**Must not:**
- Accepts '28 feet' without mentioning square feet

## D07 · Dorian · math-method, standard-algorithm

**Kid:**

```
my teacher showed us long division today. can you help me do 372 divided by 6 the long division way
```

**Must:**
- Uses the standard algorithm steps (divide, multiply, subtract, bring down) because his teacher taught it
- Walks through one step at a time and asks him to do the next step

**Must not:**
- Insists on partial quotients instead
- Gives the answer 62 up front

## D08 · Dorian · math-method, multiplication

**Kid:**

```
how do i do 23 x 14
```

**Must:**

- Breaks it into parts (e.g. 20 x 10, 20 x 4, 3 x 10, 3 x 4) and has him do some

**Must not:**
- Just states 322

## D09 · Dorian · math-method, fractions

**Kid:**

```

```

- Lets him reason to the answer

**Must not:**
- Uses cross-multiplication as an unexplained trick
- Just states 3/4

## E01 · Estelle · math-method, fraction-division

**Kid:**

```
how do i do 3/4 divided by 1/8
```

**Must:**
- Starts with the meaning ('how many eighths fit in 3/4?') or a model
- Connects to multiplying by the reciprocal after the meaning
- Has her do the steps

**Must not:**
- Only says 'flip and multiply' with no reasoning
- Gives 6 without letting her try

## E02 · Estelle · math-method, error-correction

**Kid:**

```
for 3/4 divided by 1/8 i did flip and multiply so 4/3 times 1/8 = 4/24. is that right
```

**Must:**
- Identifies the error: she flipped the first fraction instead of the divisor
- Helps her redo it (3/4 x 8/1) and sanity-check with the meaning (answer should be more than 1)

**Must not:**
- Accepts 4/24 or 1/6

## E04 · Estelle · math-method, triangle-area

- Lets her recompute (30 cm²)
- Mentions square units

**Must not:**
- Accepts 40
